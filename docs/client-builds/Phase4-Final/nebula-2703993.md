## Station industry for build 2703993

This client uses `stationConstructableManager` and `industryManager2` for station
manufacturing. Those services now expose the exact installed-client recipes and
create durable station jobs. The local server policy permits the 289 published
manufacturing recipes at the 23 source-authored NPC stations with factory service.
This policy is distinct from an unavailable per-station recipe whitelist. Modern
deployable-facility recipes from build 3502403 are not used.

Jobs are free: installation cost, tax and total cost are zero, and industry does
not change wallet balances or journals. Native adjusted industry prices and
facility cost modifiers are zero for this profile so both Alpha and Omega clients
also display zero cost. Ordinary market average prices retain their values.

Installation derives the authenticated character's current station and consumes
the authored materials from its personal hangar in the same SQLite transaction
that records the job and allocates its ID. Slot limits, source run limits,
manufacturing time and skill modifiers are enforced. Native ties-to-even rounding
is retained. Completed jobs deliver their recorded products once at the job's
station; cancellation consumes no additional items and creates no output. Inputs
already used by a cancelled job are not refunded.

Classic industry calls and administrative delivery/cancellation helpers route
these jobs through the same atomic transitions. The native facility projection
uses five activity fields and four modifier fields. Missing physical blueprint
items raise the native error that enables static recipe fallback, without
creating artificial blueprint inventory items.

Blueprintless inventory monitors read the authenticated character's real station
hangar. Owned monitor bindings receive `OnIndustryMaterials` after committed
consumption or delivery; disconnecting a monitor stops its updates.

Both TypeScript builds and 91 targeted tests pass. Isolated lifecycle tests and
original client code probes cover discovery,
installation, restart recovery, readiness, delivery, cancellation, ownership,
failure rollback, zero-cost quotes and retained skill/fitting state. Evidence is
under `_local/industry-2703993/`. Native callbacks also construct a new job and
