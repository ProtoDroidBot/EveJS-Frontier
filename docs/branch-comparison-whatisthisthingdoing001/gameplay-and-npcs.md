Active-module consequences are substantive: Leap uses authored impulse/volatility and prepays a cycle's FIFO fuel; Thrust Overdrive scales injection with online thrusters; Approach Computer requires a locked, in-range ship and revalidates it each cycle; Transfuser debits the source queue and credits the target with authored loss/capacity limits and paired-write rollback; Hull Repairer uses the shared end-of-cycle structure-repair path. Manual stopping and forced offlining have distinct cycle behavior, and releasing Leap ends thrust immediately while retaining its reactivation deadline.

Two new passive systems deserve separate attention. Fuel Blister contributes a reserve-last fuel tier; offlining seals its share on the item, onlining restores it, and removing the sealed module discards that inaccessible fuel atomically. Blackstart Cell contributes reserve capacitor capacity and fuel-free solar recharge, including on a powered-off Creation, but only in direct starlight and up to its reserve share. Shadow, warp, and berthing prevent that solar recharge.

IFF now observes effective module and hull power. Broadcast configuration persists during reversible loss of power. Beacon visibility and immobilization suspend/resume within the remaining cycle, while removal, destruction, expiration, or scene transition terminates the beacon. Verdicts distinguish matching active broadcasts (`true`), nonmatching active broadcasts (`false`), and unknown/nonbroadcasting contacts (no row). NPC faction broadcasts participate in the same comparison. The Transponder Beacon is an installed module, not a Launch Bay payload.

Known boundaries: `must_be_in_root` remains advisory because enforcing it conflicts with an authored layout; the client sends indistinguishable 50 m Approach and Keep-at-range calls, so server control authorization cannot recover which of those two UI intents produced that RPC. The passive-sensor strength-to-signal mapping is an explicit emulator approximation, not a recovered official server formula.

### Fuel, power, travel, and ship motion

[fuelTankRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/fuelTankRuntime.ts) grows from basic loading into a persistent fuel/power model. Tanks are enabled by authored regular-hull Dogma or Creation modules; an arbitrary capacity modifier no longer creates fuel capability on a tankless hull. Regular ships require an appropriate fitted engine/generator and accept its fuel family, while Creations retain supported hydrogen/crude behavior. New eligible starter hulls initialize to their current full capacity.

Fuel is persisted as ordered typed batches. Normalization preserves order and only coalesces adjacent matching batches; the head determines active fuel properties. Consumption advances through batches in FIFO order, with ordinary fuel before Fuel Blister reserve fuel, including after refueling a partially consumed queue. Fuel quality, containment, efficiency, and thermal properties influence continuous power, capacitor recharge, warp, and jump planning. Online consumer load burns fuel even when capacitor is full; only remaining power headroom recharges capacitor. Depleted fuel truncates recharge instead of granting unfunded power. Native NPC fuel consumption remains opt-in until a tank has actually been provisioned.

Warp now validates engine/fuel authority before departure and rechecks after alignment. Successful activation consumes exactly 15% capacitor once; fuel is charged during active warp rather than alignment. Dungeon destination initialization/prewarming must succeed before player or sessionless-NPC departure. Nearby site-beacon travel can fall back to approach; dungeon roots enforce a 10 km minimum warp-in stand-off, while acceleration gates retain their authored destination. These changes are in [server/src/space/destiny/commands/warpCommands.ts](../../server/src/space/destiny/commands/warpCommands.ts), `simulation/warpState.ts`, [server/src/services/dungeon/dungeonWarpSafety.ts](../../server/src/services/dungeon/dungeonWarpSafety.ts), and the scene runtime.

Adds cross-system jump-drive planning and the client RPC in [jumpDriveRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/jumpDriveRuntime.ts)/[jumpDriveMgrService.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/jumpDriveMgrService.ts). A Creation requires an online drive plus engine or generator; integrated legacy hull drives use authored `canJump`. The planner checks mass, range, current fuel, heat, and critical-temperature limits before initiation. Frontier consumption/heat use client-derived equations; the Frontier jump consumes the active FIFO batch, while legacy drive consumption uses its authored item type. NPC travel reuses this planning instead of granting fuel implicitly.

Adds [massDynamics.ts](../../server/src/space/destiny/simulation/massDynamics.ts): effective mass versus base mass scales speed and angular authority by a bounded inverse square root and acceleration by a bounded inverse ratio. Angular agility also affects commanded turns. Live motion/manual strafe reflects these limits, so heavier fittings reduce mobility rather than changing only a displayed attribute.

Dogma delivery also changes: persisted hull-damage ratios become absolute damage amounts for client attributes; live Creation modifiers are not applied twice; real module-location charges are primed; reload/unload inventory tuple quantities are updated in the correct order; fuel notifications wait until bound Dogma setup and are replayed for docked Creation initialization. A station-only zero-unit LoadFuel request fills/refreshes the authoritative remainder to tolerate transient client initialization state.

Coverage: [frontierLoadFuel.test.ts](../../server/tests/frontierLoadFuel.test.ts), [frontierJumpDriveRuntime.test.ts](../../server/tests/frontierJumpDriveRuntime.test.ts), [frontierShipMassDynamics.test.ts](../../server/tests/frontierShipMassDynamics.test.ts), [frontierDungeonWarpMaterialization.test.ts](../../server/tests/frontierDungeonWarpMaterialization.test.ts), [frontierModuleChargeNotifications.test.ts](../../server/tests/frontierModuleChargeNotifications.test.ts), plus extended Creation and Dogma tests.

### Collision, weapon authority, and physics-gun objects

The branch adds [server/src/space/destiny/collision/collisionBundle.ts](../../server/src/space/destiny/collision/collisionBundle.ts) and `simulation/collisions.ts`. Collision profiles resolve by graphic data and use transformed compound primitive geometry (spheres, capsules, boxes) where available, with bounded geometry/fallback bounds. Swept movement avoids tunneling, handles overlap/depenetration, and excludes nonphysical markers and the appropriate warp/docking/cloak phases. Client ball encoding now receives the corresponding physical/collision presentation, and undocking clears station collision geometry even when no locator is available.

[collisionImpact.ts](../../server/src/space/destiny/simulation/collisionImpact.ts) turns eligible first impacts into kinetic damage and bounded pushes. It snapshots pre-resolution velocities/masses, calculates excess closing speed above an approach-speed allowance, uses reduced mass and a speed-squared damage curve, apportions damage, and deduplicates contact episodes with a 750 ms reentry cooldown. Initial overlaps and tangent/separating contact do not create impact damage. Pushes aggregate directional contributions and require sufficient mass. Environment tuning is exposed through `EVEJS_COLLISION_DAMAGE_AT_REFERENCE`, `EVEJS_COLLISION_MAX_DAMAGE_PER_CONTACT`, and `EVEJS_COLLISION_MAX_PUSH_DELTA_SPEED`.

Weapon authority now checks the first physical obstruction. Direct fire, NPC attacks, and swept missiles cannot damage through intervening geometry merely because they have an intended target. NPC target selection/locks observe line of fire; a profile's permissive occlusion field does not bypass the shared damage gate. Victim-side combat reveal makes an unresolved attacker visible before effect delivery, while unrelated observers can receive anonymous effect endpoints rather than private target identity. Special effects use the client's fitting-slot/hardpoint key and CR module presentation uses type-and-flag pairs.

New [skillShotService.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/skillShotService.ts) and [skillShotRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/skillShotRuntime.ts) implement `BeginFire`, `TurretStateUpdate`, `EndFire`, `BeginHeldBeam`, `HeldBeamAimUpdate`, and `EndHeldBeam` with the build-3502403 acknowledgement shapes. Aimed fire resolves the first authoritative sweep without a conventional target lock. Delayed weapons use the latest accepted aim; held beams cycle, ramp, update endpoint effects, enforce cooldowns, and stop on failures. Resource costs, lens volatility, damage, and effects use shared weapon paths. Explicit Repeater automatic fire remains subject to repeated current-authority checks.

Held-beam utility routing in [skillShotUtilityRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/skillShotUtilityRuntime.ts) uses authored specialization lists. Cutting Laser/Needle can mine list-612 asteroids and salvageable-wreckage resources and otherwise retain authored combat damage. Crude Extractor handles list-601 Crude Rift resources and has no combat damage. Mining follows normal capacity/depletion/ledger transactions. An incompatible/depleted/full-cargo resource hit fails the beam rather than falling through to weapon damage; volatility is rolled once.

Physics Gun type 99999 has a distinct pickup path. [physicsGunPickup.ts](../../server/src/space/physicsGunPickup.ts) only accepts eligible nearby/scoped physical scenery/resources; it rejects stations and gameplay-sensitive dungeon gates, objectives, hazards, containers, and similar content. It durably detaches the original into a world record before replacement, transfers remaining mining state, and suppresses rematerialization of the original source. [detachedDungeonProps.ts](../../server/src/space/detachedDungeonProps.ts), [dungeonPropDetachment.ts](../../server/src/space/dungeonPropDetachment.ts), and [dungeonPropMovement.ts](../../server/src/space/dungeonPropMovement.ts) preserve pose/rotation/geometry, tether at the struck point while ship/aim move, sweep against obstacles, checkpoint before publication, and settle on release/contact. Restart restores the last durable pose. Detached world props can outlive their original dungeon teardown. GM preview/detach/move/stop commands provide a separate controlled manipulation route, and [physicsGunPoseStream.ts](../../server/src/space/physicsGunPoseStream.ts) publishes motion samples.

This does **not** implement all of [PHYSICS_GUN_PLAN.md](../../docs/PHYSICS_GUN_PLAN.md) or [DUNGEON_PROP_COLLISION_HEALTH_AND_MASS_PLAN.md](../../docs/DUNGEON_PROP_COLLISION_HEALTH_AND_MASS_PLAN.md): the latter is explicitly proposed. Comprehensive per-prop HP/mass policy, universal damageable/pushable scenery, adjustable distance/rotation, launch/free-flight/freeze distinctions, and mass-sensitive physics-gun force control remain planned. The current tether uses provisional speed/acceleration bounds and settles props rather than providing a general rigid-body sandbox.

Coverage: `frontierCollisionBundle`, `frontierSpaceCollisions`, `frontierSkillShotService`, `frontierSkillShotUtilityRuntime`, `frontierLensDurability`, `detachedDungeonProps`, `detachedDungeonPropsSqlite`, `physicsGunPoseStream`, and `dungeonPropMoveSelection` tests.

### Scanning and visibility

[scanningRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/scanningRuntime.ts) expands existing directional scanning with separate detection and object-resolution ranges, configurable SNR threshold, delayed/stable session-scoped contacts, added/removed deltas, and conversion of resolved signatures into normal ballpark contacts. Distant contacts can remain signatures or be omitted according to configuration. Combat revelation is independent of unrelated scans. Occlusion suppresses thermal information while retaining the allowed gravimetric/EM signal. Target mass affects gravity signature, temperature affects thermal signature, and active modules/weapons add decaying EM emissions.

[passiveScanningRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/passiveScanningRuntime.ts) adds periodic passive scans using effective Dogma from online sensors/support modules rather than names hardcoded in the scanner. Ion and Gravity sensor/module modifiers, Command Pod, EM Scrambler, and active Transponder bloom affect the resulting channels; offline modules and a powered-off Creation lose ordinary scan authority. Passive and directional contact ownership coexist. The runtime emits `OnPassiveScanResults(updated_scans, removed, added)` and the client's resolved-contact fate. Nonmodular hulls can use the module-less build-3502403 scanning service and their built-in sensor strengths.

Adds a separate durable remote-system intelligence subsystem: [remoteSystemScanRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/remoteSystemScanRuntime.ts), [systemScanIndex.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/systemScanIndex.ts), and [systemSignatureEventRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/systemSignatureEventRuntime.ts). Network Nodes are the current caller of a source-neutral scan contract. Survey/deep jobs select layers (sites/resources/celestials/entities), validate stargate-hop reachability/range, reserve energy, observe cooldowns, and persist progress/results/idempotency. Execution requires the claimed Sui action; completion is replay-safe. Neighboring Network Nodes receive deterministic remote-scan-detected action records. Surveys try loading a live target but can fall back to an index; deep scans fail when required loading fails. Concurrent scans share warmup inside their runtime instance, with concurrency/time/payload limits. Results aggregate rather than label player/NPC actors, omit unauthorized private content, and shed weakest aggregate rows to meet payload limits. Jump signature events persist, deduplicate, and decay per gravity/EM/thermal channel.

Remote scans do not keep systems alive forever: [sceneIdleLifecycle.ts](../../server/src/space/sceneIdleLifecycle.ts) tracks real player activity independently of startup/background/scan loads; unoccupied scenes can unload after the configured idle period (the default covered by tests is one hour). Resume placement clears collision volumes. This changes world residency as well as scan functionality.

Configuration now includes `frontierScanningDetectionRangeMeters`, `frontierScanningResolutionRangeMeters`, `frontierScanningResolutionSnrThreshold`, resolved-object/out-of-range-signature rendering flags, remote-scan enable/range/cooldown/energy settings, index/result TTLs, per-layer limits, payload size, octree depths, and warmup limits. Validation rejects a resolution range greater than detection range. The remote-scanning plan document contains forward-looking scanner possibilities; a common source-neutral interface does not mean every proposed scanner source is implemented.

Coverage: `frontierPassiveScanning`, `frontierRemoteSystemScanning`, `frontierCreationAbilities`, and `frontierSpaceCollisions` tests.

### Temperature, environmental effects, shells, death, and deployables

Adds persistent thermal simulation in [temperatureRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/temperatureRuntime.ts): a frostline/star-distance ambient model, physical solar shadows, exponential temperature response based on heat capacity/conductance, continuous heat, zero-K external warp environment, and sheltered berth/station ambient. Temperature updates persist and feed thermal scanning signatures.

[environmentalEffectsService.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/environmentalEffectsService.ts) adds server progression for heat, feralization, and temporal drift. Crude Matter Rift proximity supplies temporal exposure; configured NPC scans and damaging hits add feralization, whereas merely acquiring a lock does not. Thresholds, grace/damage delay, vitality drain, optional hull damage, and rift radius/multiplier are configurable. Eligible berthing shelters a ship. Shell/status services emit the client notifications and expose current state instead of only static compatibility responses.

[shellEquipmentRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/shellEquipmentRuntime.ts) provides real shell inventory/equipment: active shell lookup, neural-implant and raiment slots, ownership/slot checks, Dogma projection, and destruction on shell death/ascension. ExperienceService now implements correctly spelled raiment equip/delete RPCs and keeps the old `reignment` aliases. Crowns remain explicitly unsupported. New starter Creation initialization and rollback paths prevent partially created characters; selection suppresses implicit starter provisioning while clone selection is pending.

[locationSelectionMgrService.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/locationSelectionMgrService.ts) and the expanded `space/shipDestruction.ts` implement Frontier death reports and clone selection. Reports retain original hull/attacker identity, ship death and shell-only expiry have separate cleanup paths, and confirmation drives the transition rather than a timer or location-read implicitly confirming it. Selection offers eligible Refuge/hangar systems and a configured station fallback; fallback awakening starts in space rather than opening a station hangar. Shell equipment and environmental state follow the death lifecycle. [selfDestructRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/ship/selfDestructRuntime.ts) replaces stub ship RPC behavior with a 10-second client-visible timer, cancellation, and a final identity/active-hull check before shared destruction.

[refugeFittingRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/refugeFittingRuntime.ts) enables fitting near an online owned Refuge through its authored 5 km fitting component without requiring a berth. It rejects out-of-range, offline/activating, foreign, wrong-active-ship, or mismatched-interaction-scope cases.

Launch Bay now moves a real group-5142 payload from flag 184 into a persistent scoped entity, with an atomic persisted 20-second deployment/reload cooldown and rollback on failed spawning/persistence. Field Cairn keeps authored 24-hour decay. Heat Trap transfers up to 150 K from the launcher, cools with the authored recipe, becomes scoopable by its owner below 350 K, and on destruction discharges thermal area damage through shared damage/destruction attribution (including allies/owner). Field Sentry uses an authored weapon profile and a defensive attacker policy. [launchBayPayloadRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/launchBayPayloadRuntime.ts) owns payload lifecycle; [creationChargeRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/creationChargeRuntime.ts) handles launch custody.

### Dungeon, landscape, mining, and stargate content

Adds three validated root content authorities: [frontier-dungeon-spawns.json](../../frontier-dungeon-spawns.json), [frontier-dungeon-loot.json](../../frontier-dungeon-loot.json), and [frontier-landscape-spawns.json](../../frontier-landscape-spawns.json), with loaders in [server/src/config/](../../server/src/config/). Dungeon/landscape definitions generate NPC profiles/behaviors, weighted/configured encounters, formations, deterministic offsets, faction tags, hive families, loot mappings, resource carriers, and explicit site props. NPC families and exact dungeon IDs drive classification rather than a reused beacon type implying faction. Configured encounter fallback and trigger-graph compilation keep spawners reachable without treating raw trigger values as spawn counts.

The dungeon runtime/universe services now use Frontier site-group eligibility, stable per-system density, weighted celestial/resource anchors, bounded placement and overlap avoidance. Synthesized fallback templates fill missing client authority rows; exact authored templates take precedence. Raw rooms can compile initial scenery/resource content while deferred/NPC triggers retain their own lifecycle. Persistent-site reconciliation retains the oldest canonical site anchor, removes duplicates, preserves active contents/position where eligible, and replaces rejected templates. State/index updates target changed instances instead of rewriting the whole universe. Startup rotation and unfinished-pocket cleanup are explicit.

Map service now includes public/authorized private dungeon beacon rows, retains an interacted but incomplete site, and chooses the same canonical oldest anchor as scene materialization. Player entry and NPC travel initialize exact required dungeons/resource sites; arrival notifications stay scoped to the same private instance. Dungeon warp-in/room notifications match the client tuple contracts. Configured hives extend mobile-analysis-beacon linking to scenery spawners, maintaining persistent link presentation and emitting collision-safe NPC batches. Special configured encounters include Inculcator buildup and the Test Hive stress scenario; their presence in configuration should not be read as a claim that every historical NPC/content system was newly implemented.

Mining now recognizes Frontier salvageable-wreckage resource props via [frontierSalvageResources.ts](../../server/src/services/mining/frontierSalvageResources.ts), keeps carrier/ore identities and visual sizes consistent, and derives stable variable asteroid sizes and corresponding quantities. Landscape salvage resources are retained even when scenery caps apply. Depletion state survives normalization; depleted ordinary asteroids respawn after 24 hours in a live scene, while Crude Matter uses the Rift's separate lifecycle. Detached mineable props transfer quantity rather than regenerating an untouched resource. Held-beam and NPC mining reuse shared cargo/depletion/ledger paths; legacy mining and new tools remain separate where their authored families require it.

Adds durable maintenance for ordinary SDE stargates in [celestialStargateRuntime.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/celestialStargateRuntime.ts), backed by `stargateRuntimeState` and [stargate-maintenance.config.json](../../stargate-maintenance.config.json). Explicitly configured gates track dormant/ready/reactivating/active state, separate material/fuel requirements, pending-deposit reservations, idempotent receipts, and ordered signals. Unconfigured gates remain legacy-active, so introducing the feature does not disable normal map links. StargateService exposes maintenance state/actions and NPC travel can supply maintenance before transit.

### NPC identity, behavior, factions, fitting, and durable jobs

The upstream native-NPC/spawn/combat framework remains the base. The branch adds durable pilot identity, policy-driven roles/factions, physical equipment custody, specialized scan/resource behavior, and recoverable work executors.

**Identity and persistence.** [npcPilotIdentityStore.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcPilotIdentityStore.ts)/[npcPilotIdentityRuntime.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcPilotIdentityRuntime.ts) assign stable `npcCharacterID` values in reserved range 1,500,000,000–1,599,999,999, independent of disposable ship IDs. Human allocation skips that range. NPC `characterID`/`pilotCharacterID` remain zero to avoid adopting player-control semantics. Logical spawn slots distinguish system, source, group/member, landscape, dungeon/private instance, hive, and reinforcement identity; retries and later incarnations reuse the pilot where the slot/faction is unchanged. Existing durable records can migrate to a legacy entity slot. This is identity reuse by existing spawn authorities, not a new blanket respawn policy.

Native persistence now checkpoints movement, condition, fittings, cargo, and manual orders. [npcRuntimePersistence.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcRuntimePersistence.ts) adds version/checksum validation, jobs/revisions, exclusive incarnation-bound spawn leases, reservations, idempotency keys, operation journals, signal cursors, crash recovery/compensation, orphan quarantine, verified compressed snapshots, and clean shutdown/checkpoint hooks. Stale-incarnation jobs retire; disabled startup rules leave stored NPCs dormant. Native spawn/destruction/removal recover their journaled consequences and settle equipment/cargo custody instead of losing or duplicating items.

**Sui NPC identity.** [suiNpcCharacterProvisioning.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/suiNpcCharacterProvisioning.ts), [suiNpcIdentitySync.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/suiNpcIdentitySync.ts), [suiNpcProfile.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/suiNpcProfile.ts), and [suiNpcWorldConfig.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/suiNpcWorldConfig.ts) add asynchronous provisioning/reconciliation of a Character, PlayerProfile, and custom NPC profile for each durable pilot. Canonical numeric/string faction keys select deterministic faction wallets; pilots share a faction wallet without sharing Character identity. Death preserves profile identity while incarnation/current entity/death counts advance. Missing/mismatched custom profiles fail validation. Split feature implementation/type-origin/registry metadata supports stable identity across compatible upgrades. Local identity allocation can continue while chain provisioning retries. The flags `npcPilotIdentitiesEnabled` and `suiNpcCharacterProvisioningEnabled` control those two layers independently.

[suiNpcFactionFunding.ts](../../server/src/services/frontier/suiNpcFactionFunding.ts) and the funding script add idempotent minimum-budget funding from the world admin. The shipped faction budget is 10 SUI (10,000,000,000 MIST), with admin faucet retry/gas-reserve controls; it funds only deficits and never removes excess balances. This is configured Localnet development behavior, not proof of any current chain balance or deployment state.

**Roles and factions.** [npc-behavior.config.json](../../npc-behavior.config.json)/[npcBehaviorConfig.ts](../../server/src/config/npcBehaviorConfig.ts) supply guard/passive/miner/hauler policy, matching rules, default roaming/warping, and independent chase/mining/anchor capabilities while preserving authored overrides. Generic spawn-path miners enroll in mining. [npc-factions.config.json](../../npc-factions.config.json)/[npcFactionConfig.ts](../../server/src/config/npcFactionConfig.ts) add canonical identity, transponder code, allies/enemies/dispositions, retaliation, hardware policy, lane-specific SDE type membership, optional leadership/commanders, and starting geography. Starting-region selection chooses among known regional systems with configured system fallback; it is a seed, not durable territory control.

Faction transponder codes are faction-only with an optional shared suffix, not profile/group metadata; factionless NPCs do not invent a code. Identification distinguishes matching broadcasts from suspicious/unidentified contacts; aggression can escalate suspicion, and neutral factions can retaliate without becoming permanently hostile. Dungeon arrivals provoke faction-appropriate response only in the correct scope. Manual follow/lock orders can avoid weapon activation and reject cross-bubble/warping targets.

**NPC scans and metamorphosis.** [npcScanning.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcScanning.ts) adds authored inventory/metamorphosis scanner capabilities without ordinary target lock, uses appropriate type-list filters, handles native wreck storage, removes/deposits real items/traces, and applies environmental scan feralization. [npcMetamorphosis.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcMetamorphosis.ts) consumes authored metamorphosis item/amount attributes to generate the permitted cargo consequence on relevant actions. **Equipment and player management.** [npcFittingService.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcFittingService.ts), [npcFittingTrust.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcFittingTrust.ts), [npcFittingMgrService.ts](../../server/src/services/npc/npcFittingMgrService.ts), [npcCreationDraft.ts](../../server/src/services/npc/npcCreationDraft.ts), and [npcHeadlessFittingWindow.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcHeadlessFittingWindow.ts) add fitting views and mutations for conventional and Creation NPC hulls. Physical canonical item IDs/provenance remain intact across fit/unfit/charge custody. Restrictions include slots, resources, charges, compatible hull, hardware roles, faction ownership, current trust, range, and scope. Capability sets preserve dual-use weapon/mining tools. Journal recovery completes or compensates item/mirror changes; normal removal returns equipment according to policy and destruction handles legitimate losses. Headless fitting APIs can act for a validated NPC pilot, but this does not itself register strategic autonomous refitting.

The shipped faction configuration deliberately enables `debugFittingTrust.allowNearbyPlayers: true`, allowing authenticated nearby players to fit unless explicitly denied. This is a material difference from strict faction-only access. Explicit denials outrank trust, and a shareable IFF code alone is not permanent fitting authorization. The report records the committed setting, not a recommendation to deploy it unchanged.

**Limits of autonomy.** [npcDecisionTreeScaffolds.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcDecisionTreeScaffolds.ts) explicitly says its proposed Phase 6 decisions are *not registered with the live NPC behavior loop*. [npcTaskActionAdapters.ts](../../server/src/space/npc/npcTaskActionAdapters.ts) supplies guarded task preparation/navigation/self-fit/ship-swap/peer-fit adapter surfaces requiring authoritative execution ports and a faction-paid receipt; this does not establish a complete autonomous faction planner. Strategic goals, job arbitration, unified dApp command flows, live pilot ship-spawn/boarding/swap orchestration, and full resource/logistics autonomy remain planned. The documentation also identifies the piloted category-6 versus unpiloted category-11 NPC classification split as unfinished. These boundaries prevent the much larger set of plan/scaffold files from being mistaken for completed autonomous gameplay.

### Interpretation and verification limits

The branch contains both implementation and planning documents. Source comparisons establish the behaviors above and existing tests illustrate intended invariants; no server, client, game simulation, or Sui transaction was executed for this documentation pass. Claims in existing documents about a previously synchronized chain or previously passing tests are historical statements, not fresh verification. The repository-wide machine-readable file inventory should be used for exhaustive path coverage; this section groups substantive gameplay changes rather than treating generated JavaScript, TypeScript annotations, and unchanged legacy gameplay as separate new features.
